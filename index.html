Privacy Policy — CyberBoost

App: CyberBoost (com.cyberboost.gamebooster)
Effective date: 3 September 2026
Developer contact: via the developer email shown on the app's Google Play listing

Summary

CyberBoost is a gaming utility that runs on your device. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. It is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (see Advertising below); apart from the advertising identifier that AdMob uses, it does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data off your device. Everything the app reads or records stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Data collection and sharing

  • Personal & sensitive data collected: None, other than the advertising identifier described under Advertising.
  • Data shared with third parties: Only the advertising identifier and standard ad-request data, with Google AdMob, to serve ads (see Advertising).
  • Data sold: Never.
  • Analytics SDKs: None. Crash reporting SDK: Firebase Crashlytics (see Crash reporting). Advertising SDK: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent.

Files you create (screen recordings and clips) are stored in the app's media folder on your device. They are only shared if you explicitly use the in-app Share button to send them somewhere.

Data retention and deletion

All app data (settings, playtime history, recorded clips) lives only on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app, which removes all of it.

Children

CyberBoost is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Crash reporting

CyberBoost uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) so that crashes can be found and fixed. When the app crashes, a report is sent containing the stack trace, the app version, the device model and Android version, free memory and storage, and a random Firebase installation ID used to count affected devices. Reports contain no account, no name, no contacts, no recordings and no gameplay data. Crash reporting is not used in debug builds. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Advertising

CyberBoost is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob.

To show ads, AdMob receives your device's advertising ID along with standard technical information such as device type, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction events. This is handled by Google, not by us — see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any personalised ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's own Android settings at any time.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your gameplay data with advertisers. Your recorded sessions, clips, playtime statistics and the on-device insights stay on your device and are never uploaded.
